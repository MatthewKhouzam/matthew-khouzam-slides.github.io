NSERC Alliance Project

1:2 Ratio

Traditionally: $1 industry + $1 NSERC + $1 Prompt

  • The industry $1 can be split among many companies
  • Or keep all the IP generated by funding solo
  • Access to subsidized interns & grad students
  • Direct research collaboration with professors
  • Multi-year funded research program

NSERC Alliance – What We Get

  • Graduate student researchers (MSc/PhD) working on our problems
  • Undergraduate interns funded through the grant
  • Co-publication opportunities
  • Access to university labs & infrastructure
  • IP generated aligned with our roadmap

Prompt Innov

promptinnov.com

  • Quebec innovation internship facilitator
  • 50–70% wage subsidy on intern salaries
  • Connects with Quebec university/CEGEP talent
  • Focus on tech & innovation sectors

NRC IRAP – Youth Programs

nrc.canada.ca

  • Youth Employment Strategy funding
  • Subsidizes $15–25/hr for 6–12 months
  • Recent grads & students on R&D projects
  • Also provides business advisory services

Mitacs Accelerate

  • $15K per 4-month internship unit
  • Company contributes $7.5K, Mitacs matches
  • Stackable units (4, 8, 12+ months)
  • Graduate-level research interns
  • Can combine with NSERC Alliance students

NSERC Accelerate / Mitacs Alliance

2:1 Ratio

Company invests $1 → gets $2 in funded research

  • NSERC Accelerate & Mitacs Alliance provide matching funds
  • Company retains IP – unlike many grant programs
  • Used for the majority of projects in Montreal
  • Ideal for applied R&D with clear commercial outcomes
  • Combines academic research with industry-directed goals
  • Grad students work on your problems – you own the results

Tax Credits

  • SR&ED (Federal) – 15-35% refund on qualifying R&D salaries
  • Quebec R&D Credit – Additional 14-30% provincial layer
  • CDAE / AI (Quebec) – Credit for development of e-business & AI
    • Up to 30% of eligible salaries (capped per employee)
    • Covers software dev, AI, IT services
    • Administered by Investissement Québec
    • Details (PDF)
  • Interactive Digital Media Credit (QC) – Up to 37.5% of labour
  • Applied retroactively at tax time – low risk, high certainty

Grants

  • NSERC Alliance – 1:2 ratio (split costs or keep IP)
  • NRC IRAP – R&D project funding + youth placements
  • Prompt Innov – Innovation internship grants
  • Competitive but high payoff
  • Require proposals & reporting

The Montreal Advantage

  • Quebec strongly favours local operations for grants & tax breaks
  • Having a group based in Montreal unlocks:
    • Quebec-only R&D tax credits (among the most generous in North America)
    • Investissement Québec programs
    • Prompt Innov & other Quebec-funded orgs
    • Emploi-Québec wage subsidies
  • Provincial programs often require Quebec presence – we qualify
  • Municipal incentives (Montréal International, tech ecosystem support)

Why This Matters

Quebec spends ~$3.2B/year on business tax credits

  • R&D credits are refundable even without profit
  • Designed to keep tech jobs in-province
  • Our Montreal presence is a strategic asset for funding
  • Many programs are non-competitive (formula-based) – just apply

Stacking Strategy

  1. NSERC Alliance for core research + grad students
  2. Prompt Innov or Mitacs for additional interns
  3. Target ÉTS/Poly co-op for volume
  4. Claim SR&ED on qualifying work
  5. Apply to CSJ/SWPP for summer positions

Result: Effective intern cost reduced by 50–75% depending on program mix
